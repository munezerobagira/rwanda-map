The manifests record immutable source timestamps, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes.
This is an adapted database, not a census, cadastral survey or ownership register.
No contributor identities, owner names or addresses are retained.

Source geometry positional accuracy, mapping completeness and residential use
are unvalidated. Quantization is not a claim about accuracy. Building tag changes
are not verified construction/demolition. Points are representative locations
inside footprints, not footprint polygons or surveyed building positions.
